Final step of the workflow: compress the spec.

## Procedure

Rewrite the spec using tighter prose. Aim to reduce the word count as much as possible
without loosing information and with minimal impact on the structure.

Count the words. If the word count is above 300 words, compress the spec further by going
through each section and dropping points that have lowest impact and highest inferability.
Never drop the whole section!

## Output

Save the compressed version as `compressed.md`.
