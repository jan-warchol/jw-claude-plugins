Final step of the workflow: compress the spec.

## Input

The output of the previous step.

## Procedure

Rewrite the spec using tighter prose. Aim to reduce the word count as much as possible
without losing information and with minimal impact on the structure.

## Output

`compressed.md` in the process directory.
